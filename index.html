The questions a skeptical buyer asks.

How are you different from an integrator (ESN)?+

An integrator builds what you ask for, then leaves. We make your own team able to build with AI, so the skill stays in-house.

Why not just hire in-house?+

Hiring senior AI talent is slow and expensive. We bring that level now, transfer the skill, and leave your team ready to run without us. No permanent headcount.

How is this different from a Big-4 consultancy?+

A Big-4 sends a junior with a slide deck. You get senior experts who sit with your team and do the real work. Smaller, faster, hands-on.

What if we're total beginners with AI?+

That is the ideal place to start. The teams we help most already have the tools, but no one to show them how to use them well.

Is our code and data safe?+

We work inside your own environment and will sign an NDA. Nothing leaves it without your agreement, and we help you choose tools that fit your security rules.

We can have a first call within the week, and start a few days after we agree, just enough time to prepare your material.
