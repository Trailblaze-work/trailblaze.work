Hiring senior AI talent is slow and expensive. We bring that level now, transfer the skill, and leave your team ready to run without us. No permanent headcount.

How is this different from a Big-4 consultancy?+

A Big-4 sends a junior with a slide deck. You get senior experts who sit with your team and do the real work. Smaller, faster, hands-on.
